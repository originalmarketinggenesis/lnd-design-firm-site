About the Studio

Design that respects how you actually live.

L&D Design Firm is a Chennai-based architectural and interior design practice built around one idea: less clutter, more clarity. Every project — from a single office cabin to a full home turnkey build — is approached with the same discipline: minimal material palettes, functional layouts, and details that age well.

We work end-to-end, pairing design consultation with on-ground execution, so what's drawn on the floor plan is exactly what gets built.

  • Space planning & architectural floor plans
  • Turnkey interior execution, site-supervised
  • Material & finish selection (marble, veneer, laminate)
  • Design consultation for individual rooms or full homes
